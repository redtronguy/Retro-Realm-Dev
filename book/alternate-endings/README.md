# Alternate ending: The Sea That Keeps

This is a separate ending in which Edric chooses to abandon his humanity. The original novel, its ending and its EPUB remain unchanged.

## Reading order

Read the original chapters 1–29, then chapter 30, **The Chains**, through:

> *Now,* said the sea, in him. *Now, little one. I am free. Let it come.*

At that point, continue here instead of reading the remainder of the original chapter 30 or the original chapter 31:

1. [Chapter 30, alternate continuation: What He Kept](30-what-he-kept.md)
2. [Chapter 31, alternate conclusion: The Sea That Keeps](31-the-sea-that-keeps.md)

Both endings therefore share the betrayal, Cristal's founding, Edric's failures and acts of mercy, Mira's song, the breaking of the chains, and Aurelian's death. They diverge at Edric's final decision about returning the Deep's power.
