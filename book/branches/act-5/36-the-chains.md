# Event 36: The Chains

*Branches from Chapter 30, "The Chains": after the seventh chain breaks and Aurelian turns to dust, the free sea fills Edric and the wave stands up on every coast of the world, at the moment the sea says, "Now, said the sea, in him. Now, little one. I am free. Let it come."*

*The canon path, the Tidewarden ending (Given + Mercy), in which Edric says "No" and gives the whole sea back alone and is left with only a whisper of it, continues in [Chapter 30](../../30-the-chains.md) and ends in [Chapter 31](../../31-wait-and-hope.md).*

Every ending below starts from that same line. Which ones a player can reach depends on the two dials, Heart (mercy or vengeance) and Tide (given or kept), and on flags set earlier in the game.

## The endings

| Ending | Dials | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **The Tidewarden** (canon) | Given + Mercy | He gives it all back and lives on with a whisper of the sea. He parts from Mira in peace and sails west with Hayda. [Chapter 30](../../30-the-chains.md), [Chapter 31](../../31-wait-and-hope.md) |
| **The Sea That Keeps** (Codex) | Kept + Mercy | He keeps the sea so that he can protect everyone, and loses the part of himself that understood why. [What He Kept](../../alternate-endings/30-what-he-kept.md), then [The Sea That Keeps](../../alternate-endings/31-the-sea-that-keeps.md) |
| **The Flood** | Kept + Vengeance | He lets the wave come and cannot call it back. Every coast drowns, the people he loves with it, and he walks into the sea laughing, like the Warden of Ys. [36a: The Flood](36a-the-flood.md) |
| **The Hollow Count** | Given + Vengeance | He gives the sea back so that it can't spend his revenge for him, then takes the empty Sunspire as an ordinary man. He wins everything and ends up alone. [36b: The Hollow Count](36b-the-hollow-count.md) |
| **Faro's Other Way** (secret) | Given + Mercy, plus flags | Faro, kept in the sea's memory, shows him that the Return was never meant to be carried alone. The Weir-folk carry the sea home with him, hand in hand, and he survives with more of himself. [36c: Faro's Other Way](36c-faros-other-way.md) |

## How the secret ending opens

*Faro's Other Way* needs three gentle choices, made long before the chains:

- **Event 7, the bargain under the Teeth:** he asks the Deep for "only enough to live".
- **Event 10, the Tidewarden books:** he seals the forbidden chest again and never learns the binding rituals.
- **Event 35, Cassia at the gate:** Mira and the Weir-folk sing the sea-gate open (this needs Ysolde's crown, a high Heart and a low Tide). That brings forty-odd Weir-folk into the temple and keeps Cassia's hands whole.

Without all three, the player who gives everything back with a merciful heart reaches the canon Tidewarden ending instead.
