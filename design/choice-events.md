# Choice events: catalogue

**Status:** draft for the user's review. This companion to `design/choices-and-morality.md` lists new story events that give the player good and bad choices, beyond the eight big scenes taken from the novel.

**Key:**
- **Heart:** + means mercy, − means vengeance.
- **Tide:** + means taking or keeping the Deep's power, − means giving it back.
- **City** means effects on the city-builder (population, taxes, unrest, volunteers, raids).
- **Later** means a consequence that comes back in another act.
- Events marked ★ would fit naturally into a revised version of the novel, too.

Design rules for every event:
1. **No free good choice.** Mercy always costs something: time, money, power or safety. Vengeance always pays something.
2. **Consequences come back.** At least half the events have a *Later* payoff, so players feel their choices were remembered.
3. **The dark option should be tempting.** It should be rational, profitable or deserved, never cartoonishly evil.
4. **Small events can be run by city systems.** Many are built so the same template can repeat in procedurally varied forms (a beggar, a thief, a raid survivor), so the city keeps throwing up new dilemmas between story beats.

## Act I: The Betrayal (Saltmere, before the Keep)

Short, low-stakes choices that set the dials before the fall. The player doesn't know they matter yet.

### 1. The pepper casks ★
Edric finds sand in the aft hold on the voyage home.
- **Confront Dorran privately:** Heart +. Dorran is frightened and still betrays you, but in Act IV he hesitates and leaves a clue.
- **Report him to Moren:** neutral. Dorran is dismissed. Betrayal: Dorran still writes the letter, now out of hatred rather than fear, and he is crueller when you meet him in Solace.
- **Say nothing (the book's path):** the canon timeline.

### 2. The starving neighbour
Cade begs for a loan on the night you come home flush with wages.
- **Give it:** Heart +. In Act III, Cade remembers the kindness and refuses the diamond (event 14 changes).
- **Refuse:** no change. Cade's resentment deepens and he talks more freely at the Three Anchors.

### 3. Fenrick on the walkway ★
Fenrick threatens you with his hand on his sword.
- **Invite him to the wedding (canon):** Heart +.
- **Humiliate him in front of the Weir:** Heart −. He is shamed into acting faster; the arrest happens a day earlier.
- **Tell Mira's mother he threatened you:** neutral. The Weir turns on Fenrick, he enlists further away, and in Act IV his past in Saltmere is easier to dig up.

## Act II: The Keep (prison and Faro)

Choices about who Edric becomes while he has nothing.

### 4. The other prisoner
Another prisoner on the lower level, a thief named Joss, discovers your tunnel and threatens to tell the guards unless you dig him out too.
- **Bring him in:** Heart +. Digging is slower and food is shared. Later: Joss escapes with you or after you, and becomes a Cristal citizen and informant.
- **Collapse his side of the tunnel:** Heart −, Tide + (the first time you reach for the sea in anger). Later: his ghost-voice haunts the Keep when you return in Act V.
- **Report him to the guards for extra food:** Heart −. Faro is disappointed and teaches you less (lose one skill point).

### 5. Faro's medicine ★
Faro has his first stroke. The guard offers a physician's draught in exchange for the name of whoever is tunnelling.
- **Refuse:** Faro recovers more slowly and permanently loses his arm (canon).
- **Give a false name:** Heart −. Faro recovers fully and lives two years longer, so you learn one extra discipline skill. Later: an innocent prisoner is punished, and you find his bones in Act V.
- **Give your own name:** Heart +. You're moved to a deeper cell for a year and the tunnel stalls, but Faro trusts you completely and tells you the secret ending's clue early.

### 6. The first drop
Faro teaches stillness. The player can practise the slow, calm way or reach in anger.
- **Calm (minigame):** Tide −. Slower power gain, and Warden branches unlock first.
- **Anger (instant):** Tide +. Fast power, and Tide-taken branches unlock first. Faro warns you.

### 7. The bargain under the Teeth ★
The Deep offers power as you drown.
- **"For justice":** the canon terms. Starting Tide is medium.
- **"Give me everything":** Tide ++. A huge starting boost, but the Tidemark starts on your face.
- **"Only enough to live":** Tide −−. A weak start, with Warden skills discounted. The Deep finds this *interesting*, and it is the only route to the secret ending.

## Act III: The Count of Cristal (smugglers and city-building)

The longest act, and where the city-builder begins. These events double as repeatable city events.

### 8. Jac's cargo ★
The *Slip* is carrying people, not brandy: Sardhi refugees, and a slaver's agent has paid Jac to deliver them to Mazrah.
- **Free them:** Heart +. Jac loses her fee and is furious, then respects you. Later: the refugees become Cristal's first settlers (+20 population), and one of them leads you to Hayda.
- **Let the deal go through:** Heart −. You get gold for the city's founding. Later: Hayda despises you on sight in event 18.
- **Sink the slaver's ship:** Tide +, Heart ±. The refugees are freed, but the slaver's crew drowns.

### 9. The customs cutter
A King's customs cutter is about to catch the *Slip*.
- **Outrun it honestly:** you risk losing cargo.
- **Ground it on a sandbar (canon flavour):** Tide +.
- **Sink it:** Tide ++, Heart −. Later: the drowned crew's families appear in Solace, and the Radiants have a reason to hunt Cristal earlier.

### 10. The Tidewarden books ★
In the treasury you find a sealed chest marked as forbidden: rituals for binding others' will, and the method the Warden of Ys used.
- **Seal it again:** Heart +. Later: the secret ending needs it to stay sealed.
- **Study it:** Tide +, and the Compel skill unlocks early.
- **Burn it:** Heart +, Tide −. But the books are the last of their kind, and Tam (the island's schoolmaster) never forgives you.

### 11. Who may live in Cristal?
A recurring city event. Applicants arrive: a known murderer seeking a new life, a family of Radiant deserters, a debtor fleeing powerful men.
- **Admit them:** population +, unrest +, Heart +.
- **Turn them away:** stability +, Heart −.
- **Admit them under watch (spending Still Water):** Tide +, no unrest.
- Later: specific admitted characters return as quest-givers or traitors.

### 12. The blighted child ★
A raid survivor, a girl of eight, has the blight-cough. Healing her with Undertow means drawing the sickness into yourself (as at Grey Point).
- **Heal her:** Heart +, Tide +. Healing pulls in blighted water, so the Tide rises even though it's a kind act. This is the cruel trick of the Deep.
- **Send her to the hospital:** neutral. She may die (random, based on the city's hospital level).
- **Refuse because it would weaken you before Solace:** Heart −.

### 13. The night watch
Blighted things are coming ashore. Your watch is outnumbered.
- **Fight beside them (combat):** Heart +, risk of injury.
- **Slaughter the whole raid alone with Riptide:** Tide ++. Citizens stop volunteering ("the Warden will handle it").
- **Calm the creatures (Warden skill, hard):** Tide −, Heart +. Fewer creatures come next raid.

### 14. The diamond at the inn ★ (canon scene, expanded)
See the main scene list. Extra option: **give Cade the diamond and secretly pay a guard to watch him.** Heart +, gold −. The jeweller lives, Agna is caught, and Cade is spared.

### 15. Old Ysolde's crown ★
The blind grandmother gives you the wedding crown.
- **Keep it:** needed for the canon chains scene.
- **Give it to the sea now:** Heart +, Tide −. A permanent small blessing on Cristal's fishing. Later: at the chains, Mira has nothing to lay on the water, and the canon ending path becomes harder.
- **Sell it:** Heart −−. Later: Mira learns of it.

### 16. The rival smuggler-lord
The pirate king of Corrow demands tribute from Cristal, or he'll raid it.
- **Pay:** city treasury −, peace.
- **Fight:** a combat event with casualties.
- **Ruin him with Undertow** (drain his gold, turn his crews): Tide +. Later: his starving crews become raiders.
- **Offer him a place on the council:** Heart +, a risk of betrayal, and if it works, a fleet for the siege.

### 17. Bastien's vendetta ★
Bastien asks permission to go to Solace now and kill Valtor quietly.
- **Allow it:** Valtor dies early and his whole storyline is skipped. Lyse and Pip's fates change (Heloise loses her reason to poison), but you lose the trial.
- **Forbid it (canon):** neutral.
- **Promise him Valtor's ruin instead:** Heart −. Bastien becomes more loyal and more dangerous.

### 18. Freeing Hayda ★ (canon scene, expanded)
Extra option: **buy her and keep her as your weapon, without telling her she is free.** Heart −−. She testifies anyway, but as your instrument. Later: she leaves you after the Desert Witness and becomes an enemy agent in Act V.

### 19. Vasco Rook's gallows
The outlaw is about to hang in a southern town.
- **Save him (canon):** Heart +.
- **Let him hang:** Heart −. There's no Rook in Act IV, so you'll need another way to catch Dorran and Aren.
- **Save him and make him swear to stop robbing travellers:** Heart ++. Later: he refuses to imprison Dorran (event 30 changes).

## Act IV: The Three Hands (Solace)

Higher stakes, more grey choices. The tempting option is usually the cruel one.

### 20. The mirror-tower keeper ★
Hob Fennick and his dying granddaughter (canon).
- **Heal Nell first, then ask (canon):** Heart +.
- **Make the healing conditional on the false signal:** Heart −. Hob does it out of fear. Later: he betrays you to the Radiants.
- **Find another way to ruin Dorran:** slower, and needs more Undertow (Tide +).

### 21. The Courant correspondent
A journalist uncovers part of your plan and threatens to expose the Count of Cristal.
- **Bribe him:** gold −.
- **Wipe his memory with Still Water:** Tide +, Heart −.
- **Tell him the truth about the Drying:** Heart +. He publishes it, and the Radiants move against you earlier in Act V, but Solace's people are on your side.

### 22. Elsabet's escape
Dorran's daughter begs you to help her escape the marriage to Andric.
- **Help her (fund her journey south):** Heart +. Later: she sends warning of Dorran's flight route.
- **Use her as leverage against Dorran:** Heart −, Dorran is ruined faster.
- **Ignore her:** canon.

### 23. Mira's night visit ★ (canon scene, expanded)
- **Tell her everything (canon):** Aren withdraws the challenge.
- **Lie and let the duel happen:** you must choose to let Aren shoot you (Heart +++, you are wounded and the Tide spikes) or shoot him (Heart −−−, and Mira's path closes forever).
- **Use Still Water to make her forget you visited:** Tide +, Heart −. She never knows you live.

### 24. The stillroom ★ (canon pivot)
See the main scene list: **whether Pip lives.** Extra option: **tell Heloise, then sabotage her supply** (a stealth mission). Heart +, risky. If you fail, the canon tragedy happens.

### 25. Andric's offer
The Pale Knight works out who you are and offers a partnership: he'll ruin Valtor publicly if you help him escape afterwards.
- **Accept:** the trial happens your way, but Andric escapes and becomes a recurring villain in later content.
- **Refuse and expose him:** canon.
- **Kill him quietly:** Heart −−. Bastien never forgives you (he dug the boy up).

### 26. The poisoned well
To bring down Dorran's grain monopoly, an agent suggests spoiling his warehouses. A poor district gets its grain from them.
- **Do it:** Dorran is ruined faster. Heart −−. A famine event hits Solace's poor quarter.
- **Undercut his prices with Cristal's trade instead:** slower, and costs Cristal's treasury. Heart +.

### 27. Fenrick's soldiers
After the Desert Witness, Fenrick's veterans are unpaid and rioting.
- **Pay them out of your fortune:** Heart +, and they join Cristal's defence in Act V.
- **Let the riots burn:** chaos in Solace, and the Radiants step in harder.
- **Crush the riot with Riptide:** Tide ++, and the King notices you sooner.

### 28. Valtor's madness
Valtor, mad, digs in his garden. You can end his suffering, heal his mind (Undertow, very costly), or leave him.
- **Heal him:** Tide +, Heart ++. He confesses everything publicly and dies sane, which brings a Chamber reform event.
- **End it:** Heart −. Lyse never visits his grave.
- **Leave him (canon).**

### 29. Severin's request ★
The paralysed old man asks you, letter by letter, to end his life before his son's household destroys his granddaughter.
- **Refuse, and protect Lyse instead (canon):** Heart +.
- **Grant it:** Heart ±. Lyse grieves earlier, but the Ebb's old network trusts you completely.

### 30. Dorran in the cave ★ (canon scene, expanded)
Extra option: **forgive him and send the money back to the towns anonymously (canon)**, versus **forgive him but keep the money for Cristal's walls** (Heart +, Tide neutral, City +). The towns' beacons stay dark, and Act V raids hit the mainland harder.

## Act V: The Sun King

Fewer, heavier choices. Everything earlier comes back here.

### 31. The King's offer ★
In the Sunspire, the King offers to share the drinking.
- **Refuse (canon).**
- **Pretend to accept:** you get inside his defences, Tide ++ (you drink once), and the King's trust lets you reach the Lock with fewer enemies. The taste of it raises your Tide permanently.
- **Accept for real:** a hidden fifth "dark" path, the Twin Kings. A short alternate Act V where you become his heir. A game-over-style ending.

### 32. The race to Cristal
The fleet is ten days ahead and Lyse has forty days.
- **Drive the ship with the sea:** Tide ++, you arrive in time.
- **Send Lyse's waking instructions ahead to a Cristal child who can hear the sea:** needs a low Tide and a Cristal school (city level). Heart +. You arrive late, but the child wakes her.

### 33. Olaf's temple ★
In the siege, you can save Olaf by leaving the harbour wall undefended.
- **Save Olaf:** the harbour falls and the Weir houses burn.
- **Hold the wall (canon):** Olaf dies singing.
- **Spend Tide to do both:** Tide ++.

### 34. The fleet ★ (canon scene, expanded)
Lift the ships (canon), drown them, or **lift them and keep the flagship's mirrors intact as a weapon** (Tide neutral, Heart −). You can turn sunfire against the King later, at a cost.

### 35. Cassia at the gate ★
Cassia offers to burn her hands to open the sea-gate.
- **Accept (canon):** she loses her hands.
- **Refuse and break the gate yourself:** Tide +++, and you arrive at the chains half-consumed.
- **Let Mira and the Weir-folk sing it open:** a secret option. It needs Ysolde's crown, a high Heart and a low Tide. It's the doorway to the secret ending.

### 36. The chains ★ (canon scene)
The four endings (and the secret fifth), decided by the dials and by event flags from earlier.

## How events feed the endings

The ending is not decided only at the chains. The final choice is shaped by what you've done:

| Earlier choices | Effect at the chains |
|---|---|
| Kept Ysolde's crown (15) and had a high Heart with Mira (23) | Mira can sing; the "give it back" option is available even at a high Tide |
| Studied the forbidden binding books (10) | The "keep it" option gains the Compel flavour (the Sea That Keeps becomes harsher) |
| Saved Pip (24) | The Deep asks *why* you stopped; you can answer, and Tide pressure drops |
| Drowned the fleet (34) or the customs crew (9) | The Flood option can't be refused unless Heart is very high |
| Sealed the books (10) + "only enough to live" (7) + the Weir-folk gate (35) | **Secret ending** unlocked |
